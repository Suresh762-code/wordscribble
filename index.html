FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Word Scribble free?

Yes. Word Scribble's basic word and text tools are free to use.

Do I need an account?

No. You can use the basic Word Scribble tools without creating an account.

Does Word Scribble store my text?

The core tools are designed to process text directly in your browser where possible. See the Privacy Policy for additional information.

Can I use Word Scribble on mobile?

Yes. Word Scribble is designed to work on modern phones, tablets and desktop browsers.

What is a word scrambler?

A word scrambler changes the order of letters in a word or phrase to create new letter combinations.

What is an anagram?

An anagram is created by rearranging the letters of a word or phrase to produce another arrangement using the same letters.

Can I scramble a phrase?

Yes. The Word Scrambler accepts short phrases and includes an option to preserve spaces.

What can I use the Random Word Generator for?

Random words can be useful for creative writing, brainstorming, games, classroom exercises and vocabulary activities.
