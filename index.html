What is a word scrambler?

A word scrambler rearranges the letters in a word or phrase into a new order. It is useful for classroom activities, word games, puzzles, vocabulary practice, brainstorming and creative writing.

How to use Word Scribble

  1. Enter a word or short phrase.
  2. Choose how many scrambled results you want.
  3. Optionally preserve spaces or the first and last letters.
  4. Select Scramble Word.
  5. Copy any generated result with one click.

Why use Word Scribble?

All core tools run directly in your browser. No account is required, and your input does not need to be sent to a server for the basic tools to work.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Is Word Scribble free?

Yes. The tools on this website are designed to be free to use.

Can I scramble multiple words?

Yes. You can enter a short phrase and keep spaces preserved while the letters are randomized.

Does Word Scribble store my text?

The core tools run locally in your browser and do not require your text to be stored on a server.

Can I use it on mobile?

Yes. The site is responsive and works on modern phones, tablets and desktop browsers.
