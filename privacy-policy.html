Your Privacy

Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Word Scribble handles information when you use our website and online tools.

Last updated: September 16, 2026

1. Introduction

Word Scribble respects your privacy. We aim to keep our word and text tools simple while collecting only the information reasonably necessary to operate, maintain and improve the website.

By using Word Scribble, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

2. Information You Enter Into Our Tools

Word Scribble provides tools such as a Word Scrambler, Word Unscrambler, Anagram Generator, Random Word Generator, Word Counter and text transformation tools.

The current core tools are designed to process the text you enter directly in your browser. Word Scribble does not require you to create an account to use these basic tools.

3. Information We May Collect Automatically

When you visit Word Scribble, certain technical information may be collected automatically by hosting, analytics or similar services used by the website. This may include information such as:

  • Browser type and version
  • Device type
  • Operating system
  • Pages visited
  • Approximate visit times and usage information
  • Referring pages or websites
  • General diagnostic and performance information

This information may be used to understand website traffic, maintain security, identify technical problems and improve Word Scribble.

4. Cookies and Similar Technologies

Word Scribble and services used by the website may use cookies or similar technologies for functionality, analytics, security, preferences or advertising.

Cookies are small pieces of information that websites can store through your browser. You can control or delete cookies through your browser settings. Disabling certain cookies may affect some website functionality.

5. Analytics

Word Scribble may use analytics services to understand how visitors interact with the website. Analytics can help us understand information such as popular pages, general traffic patterns, device types and website performance.

If analytics services are enabled, those providers may process information according to their own privacy policies and applicable settings.

6. Advertising

Word Scribble may display advertisements in the future. Advertising providers may use cookies or similar technologies to deliver, measure and improve advertising, subject to applicable laws and consent requirements.

If advertising services such as Google advertising products are enabled, additional information about their data practices and available privacy controls may be provided through this Privacy Policy or an appropriate consent mechanism.

7. Third-Party Services

Word Scribble may rely on third-party services for functions such as website hosting, analytics, performance monitoring or advertising.

These third parties may process information under their own privacy policies. Word Scribble does not control the privacy practices of third-party websites or services.

8. External Links

Word Scribble may contain links to external websites. If you follow a link to another website, that website's own privacy policy and terms will apply.

We encourage you to review the privacy practices of external websites before providing personal information.

9. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to maintain the security and reliability of Word Scribble. However, no website, internet transmission or electronic storage method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

10. Children's Privacy

Word Scribble is a general-purpose word and text utility website and is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children through its core tools.

If you believe personal information has been provided inappropriately, please contact us so the matter can be reviewed.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when Word Scribble changes, when new services are introduced, or when privacy practices or legal requirements change.

When this policy is updated, the "Last updated" date at the top of this page will be revised.

12. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or Word Scribble's privacy practices, please contact us through our Contact page.
